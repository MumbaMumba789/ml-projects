CACB_deposits_prospects.shp - A point shapefile representing sediment-hosted copper deposits, prospects and grouped deposits for the Central African Copperbelt, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and Zambia.

CACB_permissive_tracts.shp - A polygon shapefile representing sediment-hosted copper permissive tracts for the Central African Copperbelt, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and Zambia.

CACB_open_pits.shp - A polygon shapefile representing the areal extent of sediment-hosted copper ore bodies for the Central African Copperbelt, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and Zambia.

CACB_orebodies.shp - A polygon shapefile representing sediment-hosted copper open pit mines for the Central African Copperbelt, in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC) and Zambia.

GIS_references.xlsx - Excel file that relates short citation to full reference.

Unit_look_up_table.xlsx - An Excel spreadsheet that relate map unit abbreviations with map unit names.
